Piano samples — notes/*.ogg (29 natural notes, C2 through C6)
Salamander Grand Piano v3 by Alexander Holm; recorded Yamaha C5 grand piano.
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
Original distribution: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Source revision: 3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404
Source distribution/FLAC remapping: kinwie and the sfzinstruments contributors.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Full legal code is bundled in licenses/SALAMANDER-CC-BY-3.0.txt and displayed below.
BirdNote changes: selected velocity layer 8; downmixed lossless FLAC recordings to
mono; trimmed leading silence; shifted nearest recorded pitches by at most one
semitone to provide every natural note; resampled to 44.1 kHz; applied common gain;
shortened to 2.5 seconds with a 0.25-second fade; encoded as OGG Vorbis quality 4.
